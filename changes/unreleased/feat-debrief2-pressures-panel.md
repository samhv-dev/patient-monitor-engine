### Added

- **A "Pressures" panel among the Debrief's trend charts (the owner's ruling of 6 November, question 2 = B)**: after
  ART M / NIBP M, in the same size, frame and print scale as the other charts, it draws the systolic and diastolic as
  two lines on the left axis and the CVP dashed on its own right-hand axis (a shared 0–200 mmHg axis would draw a CVP of
  5–20 mmHg as a flat line); the left axis keeps MAP's 40 mmHg least span, the right a 10 mmHg one. A cuff reading,
  which the record keeps only in its cycle's second, is a dot at its cycle with gaps between, never a line to zero; an
  arterial run is a line. A file recorded before the pressures were kept draws no panel, and the trends say once
  "SBP, DBP and CVP: not recorded in this file." The Persian handout script draws the same panel (its two caption
  strings print in English until translated).
