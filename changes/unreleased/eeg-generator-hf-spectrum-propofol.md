  general-anaesthesia depth the generator's frontal spectrum kept an f^−1.7 tail above 20 Hz, where the measured frontal
  spectra of propofol anaesthesia fall ≈ f^−4 (Akeju 2014 Fig. 3C; Purdon 2015 Fig. 3A). Above 25 Hz the background now
  takes the measured exponent (4.2, with the elderly's flatter exponent from the same age term as before), coming on
  with the frontal alpha through loss of responsiveness, for the propofol-class share only; awake and sedation are
  unchanged. The numbers come from the papers' spectra, not from any device's index. The BIS-type reader's course now
  reads 51 at surgical depth (was 86) and 64 at loss of responsiveness (was 83), so its C26 and C27 expected failures
  are removed; C28 (the age paradox) still misses. New tests set the generator against the three papers, with four
  documented misses whose causes lie outside the high-frequency fall (the awake spectrum, the sedation beta, the beta
  shoulder at depth).
