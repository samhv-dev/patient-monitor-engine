### Changed

- **A TCI channel runs no faster than the pump family's maximum for its syringe.** The TCI pump used to run at up to
  1200 mL/h whatever syringe was loaded, a figure no document supports. It now runs at most at the highest rate the
  Mindray BeneFusion SP5 family's operator's manual gives for the loaded syringe: 1500 mL/h with a 50 or 60 mL
  syringe, 600 with 30 mL, 400 with 20 mL, 200 with 10 mL and 100 with 5 mL. That manual is the plain SP5's, not the
  TCI edition's, and the data says so; its maximum stands in until the TCI edition's manual is read. A propofol
  induction on a 50 mL syringe reaches its target sooner, and on a 20 mL syringe later, than before. The TCI face
  states the limit for the loaded syringe. A TCI target is refused on a channel with no syringe loaded.
- **The SP5 family's other readings as data.** The TCI pump's profile records the family manual's rate step, bolus
  and purge rates, KVO, occlusion levels, alarm list and signals, and volume and time display ranges, each with its
  page and marked as the family's number. They change nothing a channel does.
