### Added

- **A learner's phone opens the learner remote with the learner PIN (plan learner-action-remote.md L6, §4 D1, D9;
  LQ1).** The instrument picker has a card "Learner remote". Its line is the plan's draft, and the owner rules the
  words. Its "Show here" opens `#/remote/learner` on the same page. That page first asks for the learner PIN the
  instructor reads out, in the instructor phone's form (its keypad and its words, with "learner PIN" in them). The PIN
  is held in the page's memory only: never in the address, the history or the phone's store. At sign-in the phone
  says hello as `learner` with its device key and the PIN, and the laptop grants it on the learner PIN. Signed in,
  the phone shows the patient's monitor as a strip, three tabs (Drugs, Infusions, Labs) and the connection line with
  "Change" back to the picker. The tabs are 44 px targets and say that their menus are not in this release yet (L7,
  L8 and L11 fill them). A wrong learner PIN, too many tries, a full room, an older kit and a learner PIN changed on
  the laptop are each said in words, and the form comes back empty.
- **Room setup shows the learner PIN beside the instructor's.** It sits behind the same press-and-hold, so it is not
  in the page until held. "New learner PIN" draws a new one and signs every learner remote out. The panel counts the
  learner remotes signed in and the wrong learner PINs of the last minute. The device table names a learner remote
  "Learner remote", "signing in" until the laptop grants it.
