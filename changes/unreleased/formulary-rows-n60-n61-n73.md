### Fixed

- **The preparation bench's formulary: three rows that made it say something untrue** (brief 152; N60, N61, N73).
  A compatibility or admixture statement can name the containers it holds for (`containers`): Trissel's insulin loss
  in 0.9 % sodium chloride, measured in PVC bags, is now a finding for a PVC bag and no longer for a syringe. Sodium
  bicarbonate carries a sourced molar mass (84 mg per mmol, the Hospira 8.4 % label on DailyMed), so a bicarbonate
  preparation in mmol/mL finds its Stabilis stability row in mg/mL; a drug without one still finds none, and the
  reason is named. A reconstituted powder holds the volume its label states (`reconstitute.final`): Agilus 120 mg is
  22.6 mL at 5.31 mg/mL after 20 mL of water (EMC SmPC), not 20 mL at 6 mg/mL, so a weight-based dose is drawn as
  about 13 % more volume; where the label states neither (Dantrium IV 20 mg) the bench counts the 60 mL added and
  says that it ignores the powder's own volume.
