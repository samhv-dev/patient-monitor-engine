### Added

- **A learner starts, changes and stops infusions from the phone (plan learner-action-remote.md L8; LQ4).** The
  learner remote's Infusions tab lists every infusion running on the patient, whoever started it: the instructor, the
  case card, another learner remote or this phone. Each shows its rate in mL/h, the dose rate it gives this patient
  (for example "26 µg/kg/min for 64 kg"), and "set by … at mm:ss" from the instructor's computer's own record of the
  last rate set. An infusion that was already running when the phone signed in says so instead of naming anyone. Under
  them, every ready syringe on the tray can be started at a rate in mL/h. Beside the rate the phone shows the dose rate
  from the instructor's computer's concentration and the patient's weight. On every running infusion, "Change" sets a
  new mL/h and "Stop" stops it. The instructor's computer keeps that infusion's own syringe and records the phone and
  the rate it replaced. A refusal is shown in the instructor's computer's own words. A target-controlled pump is
  listed without Change and Stop.

### Known limitations

- **The phone names another learner remote by the first four characters of its device key.** The names given on Room
  setup are kept on the laptop only, so the record names the device and the phone does not.
