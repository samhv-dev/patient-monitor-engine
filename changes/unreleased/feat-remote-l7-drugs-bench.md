### Added

- **A learner prepares and gives drugs from the phone (plan learner-action-remote.md L7; LQ3, LQ7, LQ11).** The
  learner remote's Drugs tab holds the preparation bench over the tray. The bench finds a drug by name among the drugs
  that have a formulary entry, then shows its stocked products in the label's words (strength, form, the
  reconstitution volume). The learner chooses how much (vials, or mL drawn), the diluent and its volume, and the
  container, and may add another drug to the same syringe. The label's listed diluents come first and every other
  diluent is offered too. The bench shows what the syringe then holds, as a preview on the phone, and the preparation
  time from its source, or "Preparation time: no source" where the formulary gives none. "Put on the tray" sends the
  choice; the instructor's computer makes the preparation from the formulary and its answer is the concentration the
  tray shows. The tray shows each syringe preparing, with its countdown on the case clock, or ready, and what is left.
  "Give" gives all of it or a part, by IV, IO, IM or SC, over a number of seconds. "Deliver the tray" gives every
  ready syringe at once. A refusal is shown in the instructor's computer's own words ("not ready yet (0:42)", "given
  4 s ago from the laptop"). A restart or a bookmark restore on the laptop clears the tray. Nothing on the phone warns
  of a wrong diluent or a mix. When the instructor shows the incompatibility notice, the phone sounds once and shows
  the finding with its source inside the preparation. The instructor's control for it is L9's; this release has the
  host's side of it.
