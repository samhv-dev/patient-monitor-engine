### Changed

- **CYP450-cleared drugs slow by the sourced rate in hypothermia** (research/44 L2-27): midazolam, ketamine, fentanyl,
  sufentanil, lidocaine, bupivacaine and ropivacaine lose 11.1 % of their clearance per °C below 36.5 °C. Source:
  Hostler 2010, midazolam as the CYP3A4/5 probe, which falls inside the 7–22 %/°C that Tortorici 2007 gives for
  CYP450-metabolised drugs. At 33 °C the factor is 0.61, where it was 0.81. Other pathways (glucuronidation, esterases,
  renal, Hofmann) have no source and keep the −5 %/°C placeholder.
