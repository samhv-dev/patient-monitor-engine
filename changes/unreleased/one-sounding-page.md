### Changed

- **One sounding page per session on one computer.** The instructor's page, a learner monitor window and a
  defibrillator window each play the whole monitor's sound from their own engine, about 0.1 s apart, so two of them on
  one computer were heard as a double beep. Among the pages of one browser that follow one session, only the page last
  switched on now sounds: the page that was sounding switches off and its Sound switch says where the sound went
  ("Sound off: playing in the learner monitor"). A page that closes or reloads gives the sound up and no other page
  takes it over without a press. Pages in another browser or on another device (a phone) keep their own sound. Tones,
  levels, timing, Silence and alarm pause are unchanged; the ventilator's own tones are outside the rule.
