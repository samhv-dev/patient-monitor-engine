### Added

- **The scenario builder's last sections (plan `docs/plans/scenario-builder.md` §5.2, piece B5).** Four new sections in
  the builder's outline, so every block of the `pme-scenario/2` format is now edited in the form. **Script** ("card-set,
  never graded"): each entry's id, the anchor it fires at (a phase's entry, the nth taking of an action, a time after the
  start), its commands in the app's words (reordered or removed; the last one stays) and new ones from the action editor's
  command form, now shared (`commandForm`), with the card-set events the library's scripts use (a condition, the Drug
  sheet's blood loss, a rhythm). **Outcomes**: words, id, a truth rule (the phase editor's rule tree), the anchor its
  window opens at (the start, a phase, an action, the pulse loss, ROSC, or a truth rule) and the window's seconds, with
  their sources; a new outcome is written only complete. **Debrief hints**: the lines of what the case is built to show and
  the openers, each timed from a run event. **Handout questions**: each timed as an opener is, with a live count against
  the 92 characters of one printed A4 line; a 93rd character is refused by the validator with its own line and Export
  waits. One anchor picker serves all three places and offers only this card's own phases and actions. The schema's limits
  (4 take-home lines, 4 lines to show, 4 openers, 3 questions) stop the form's Add, read from the schema. Lossless: every
  library card, and a copy of the MH card carrying outcomes and debrief hints, drawn with every control touched, is
  written again byte for byte.
