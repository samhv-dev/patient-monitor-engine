- **The SP5 TCI's device data from its maker's brochure.** The TCI pump's profile now records what the Mindray
  BeneFusion SP5 TCI brochure states, each value with the brochure's page: the four drugs and seven models, which
  models take a plasma target only, the concentrations, the target ranges with their units, the age column, the
  syringe sizes and the accuracy. Data only: what a TCI channel offers and refuses is unchanged. Every mechanical
  number (the TCI rate ceiling included) stays a named gap until the pump's operator's manual is read.
- **A TCI model by its author's surname.** The pump face, the Log and the Debrief write "Schnider", not the set id
  `schnider`, and a target with its unit ("Ce target 3 µg/mL").
- **No exponent form for a pump or remote number.** A residue rate printed "3.9e-12 mL/h"; the pump face's number form
  is now plain notation, and the learner remote writes its rates and dose rates with it.
