### Added

- **The laptop sees every lab result of the case (plan learner-action-remote.md L15; LQ13).** Instructor v2's dock has a
  new last tool, "Labs". Its sheet lists every sample the case has drawn, the latest first, with who drew it: the
  learner remote by the name Room setup gave it, the instructor, the instructor's phone or the scenario card. A sample
  still in the analyser reads as the phone reads it ("Result expected mm:ss", with the analyser or the laboratory, its
  time only); a returned one shows the same table the learner's phone shows, with the same ranges, arrows and
  "critical" marks. The sheet only shows results that an analyser has returned: the simulator's continuous laboratory
  values are never shown in it. Nothing is drawn from the sheet. A restart clears the list; a return to a bookmark keeps
  the samples drawn before the bookmark, and a result returned after it is pending again until the analyser returns it.

### Known limitations

- **The instructor has no sample button on the laptop.** Whether the instructor should draw samples from the laptop has
  not been decided; until then the sheet only lists them.
- **The sheet is in English only**, as the rest of Instructor v2.
