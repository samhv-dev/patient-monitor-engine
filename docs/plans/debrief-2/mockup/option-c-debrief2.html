Debrief hints for this scenario from the scenario file placeholder wording, not reviewed by the owner

What this case is built to show
  • Tamponade is an obstructive shock: tachycardia, a narrow pulse pressure and a raised CVP come before the arrest.
  • Induction drugs can remove the compensation that keeps the pressure up.
  • In PEA from tamponade, compressions alone do not refill the heart: decompression is the treatment.
Openers for the analysis (advocacy-inquiry)
  • "I saw the systolic go below 80 at 06:13 [moment 2]. I'm curious what you were weighing then."
  • "The drainage came 1 min 30 s into CPR [moment 5]. What would have let it come before 07:48?"

Take-home and next steps placeholder wording from the scenario hints

  • Narrow pulse pressure + raised CVP + tachycardia after chest trauma: think tamponade, plan drainage early.
  • Before induction in a compensating patient, ask what keeps the pressure up.
  • Practise: run this case again from the bookmark at arrival and aim to drain before the pulse loss.
