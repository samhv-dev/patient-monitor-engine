### The ten most consequential

1. **Autonomic hub missing** (L2-01–L2-04, T-01, DEV-05). Four parallel gain paths stand in for one autonomic outflow: the humoral arm, the sympathetic stress gains, the baroreflex and the chemoreflex. The baroreflex HR gain is 0.04/mmHg against its cited 0.01–0.02. These paths carry 20 expected failures and every haemorrhage row: a 50 % bleed settles at MAP 63.
2. **Arterial timing** (V-01, V-02, T-19). The electromechanical delay is `EMD_S = 0`, and the radial "notch" the detector finds is the trough of a 5 Hz resonator, not the incisura. In the V1 rows the engine reads 95–107 ms against VitalDB's 150–171 ms. The engine-core acceptance test's floor was lowered from 150 to 110 ms to fit. The miss distorts every ABP waveform: 4 amber rows and 7 red ones.
3. **Arrest, CPR and low flow** (T-03, L2-05, L2-15). VF, asystole and ROSC come from hazard timers and weights on K, temperature and catecholamines, with no electrophysiological substrate. 17 expected failures result: CPP above the Paradis band, the EtCO2 course, no ROSC. No brief exists.
4. **EtCO2 low-flow τ 70 s** (TIME-01 = L2-19). PR #25 fitted it to the arrest fall. On main it now governs the ROSC *rise*, and research/03 §4.6 states that rise is abrupt (≥ 10 mmHg within 2–4 breaths). The mechanism needed is CO2 delivery by pulmonary blood flow.
5. **The calibration pass is unapplied** (V-20). 172 band proposals have no rulings ledger. 67 of them would turn misses into passes, so each proposed edge must be checked against its source, not against the measurement, before the owner rules. Spot check: 8 of 10 follow a primary source, and 2 carry a width the authors chose.
6. **Haemorrhage HR bands are wrong** (V-07). Six amber rows come from the ATLS HR column. Mutschler 2013 (n 16,305) gives ED HR 86 / 90 / 96 / 97 across classes I–IV. Correct these bands to the source **before** item 1, or the hub will be tuned to wrong physiology.
7. **Regional flow and lactate are fitted to the class-III lactate band** (L2-09). 60 % of VO2 turns supply-dependent along a CO ratio ramp of 0.88 → 0.55, and hepatic flow is (CO/CO0)². The renal pair comes next (L2-06, L2-07): urine composition fixed at Na 100 / K 50 mmol/L, an EABV exponent of 0.35 fitted to one check, and a second angiotensin state.
8. **Lung pathology magnitudes** (X-03, T-26, T-27). About 35 effect sizes have only their direction sourced: the effusion compliance step per litre, the tension ceiling of 25 mmHg, the ARDS PVR multipliers. On top of that, the signature test adds an unsourced tolerance (± 10 %, shunt ± 0.03, SpO2 ± 1) and an 11-band `KNOWN` allow-list that no lister counts.
9. **Showcase bands re-centred on the engine** (V-24 = T-22). The tamponade `sysMaxAtRosc` has been re-centred four times (155 → 199 → 208.5 → 191 → 210.8). Of the last +19.7 mmHg, 10.9 mmHg is the arterial beat detector missing feet during compressions, a device defect now written into the band.
10. **Scripted post-shock recovery and the NIBP envelope** (TIME-12, X-07). After a terminating shock, HR and BP follow random-length linear ramps on MANUAL targets, even in MODELED physiology. The NIBP oscillation is tanh(PP/50) × 4 mmHg on a Gaussian, standing in for the arterial P–V physics under the cuff (Drzewiecki 1994).

### Proposed correction order

1. **Stop the inflow (hours).** Split the tag into `[ENG]` (truly non-physiological) and `[FIT: target]` (L2-61), and add a CI rule that a `[FIT]` must name a source or a mechanism-brief id. Fold the 205 variant tags into one of the two. Correct the stale "158" sentence in known-deviations.md (the tree has 155).
2. **Un-soften the tests (hours each).** T-19: back to the 150 ms floor, as an `it.fails` carrying the measured number. T-18: the COPD hyperinflation test goes back to `it.fails` until a sourced number exists. T-26/T-27: the lung tolerance becomes the catalogue band itself, and the `KNOWN` list joins the lister. T-22/V-24: the tamponade pin goes back to its sourced or physiological value, and the detector defect (research/41 #50) gets its own row.
3. **Correct the mis-specified bands to their sources (half-day).** V-07 (Mutschler 2013 for the haemorrhage HR), then the calibration-pass proposals (V-20). Rule each one only after its edge has been checked against its own source. The amber rows the validation sweep calls "band or rig mis-specified" (12) go in the same pass.
4. **Autonomic hub (stage)**, items 1 and 7: one outflow with baroreflex, chemoreflex and humoral inputs, and regional flow driven by it. This retires most of T-01, DEV-05, L2-01–04 and L2-09.
5. **Arterial timing (stage)**, item 2: a sourced EMD and aorta → radial transit, and wave reflection in place of the resonator. The ABP regression rows are re-recorded in the same PR.
6. **Arrest and low-flow CO2 (stage)**, items 3, 4 and 10a: CO2 delivered by pulmonary blood flow, an arrest and ROSC substrate, and post-shock contractility and rate from the state the engine already carries.
7. **Cheap class-A replacements (hours each).**
   - ondansetron QTc (X-05): +5.6 ms at 8 mg on the label against the engine's +14 ms at 4 mg;
   - cardioversion success (X-08): Mittal 2000, 68 % at the first 70 J shock;

   - hypothermic clearance (L2-27): Tortorici 2007, 7–22 %/°C;
   - the lipid-emulsion row (X-06), which contradicts its own Miller citation.
8. **Then the per-organ mechanism briefs:** renal (L2-06/07/08), lung pathology families (X-03), NIBP cuff physics (X-07), the condition profiles that never reach their premise state (V-11 RV infarct, V-13 AS, V-16 HFrEF; sources first, since their bands are tables-only), and the remaining B rows by consequence.
9. **Last, tagging:** the D rows get their citation beside the value; the A\* rows go to a literature search; the C rows stay as they are.

**Scope and counting.** The command in the task returns **402** lines that carry the exact `[ENG]` tag, in 101 files.
Every one of the 402 is assigned to exactly one row below; the raw count each row covers is given in brackets in
"what it is". There are also **205 more lines tagged with a variant** (`[ENG, fit …]`, `[ENG fit]`, `[ENG; …]`,

induction"). They are not counted. I folded the consequential ones into the matching row and marked them "+variant".
Whoever owns the 710 total should check whether that number includes the variant tags.

**Two distinctions used in the classes.**
- Fitting a parameter of a mechanism that is already modelled, against measured data, is parameter identification.
  It is not a missing coupling. One example is the Starling capillary Kf fitted to Hahn's volume kinetics. Such rows
  are A or D.
- B means a gain, lookup, ramp or threshold that does the job of a physiological link the engine does not model.
  A\* means the value is a parameter of a real mechanism, but this audit found no source for it. A\* is still class A
  for counting. Its correction is a literature search, and nothing better is proposed here.

**MANUAL vs MODELED.** `hemo/` and `gas/coupling.ts` mostly serve the instructor-driven MANUAL mode, where the
pressures are scripted. A gain there is lower priority than the same gain in the MODELED circulation. Rows say which
mode they belong to.

**EEG.** There is no EEG code in this checkout. `l2/brain/` has no EEG constants at `0e547156`; the EEG stage is
PRs #318/#320/#322, which are not in this base. So the owner's 7 Nov ruling is recorded as a pointer row (L2-45) and
was not researched again.

**Top 5 (most consequential).**
1. **L2-01 humoral haemorrhage arm.** The sizes are fitted to whether named scenarios arrest or "collapse". It decides survival of the bleeding patient at induction. Its 30 % of blood volume moved into unstressed volume has no source.
2. **L2-02/L2-04 sympathetic and chemoreflex.** Four parallel gain paths (endo stress gains, endo hypercapnia, adrenal drives, circ chemoFactors) stand in for one autonomic outflow. The code admits the overlap and calls folding it "not done". Every hypercapnic, MH and shock HR/MAP band rides on these paths.
3. **L2-03 baroreflex.** The HR gain is 2× its cited range and the SVR gain is above its range. Fixed asymmetry multipliers replace the sigmoid. It sets every induction, haemorrhage and pressor response.
4. **L2-05/L2-15 arrest and ectopy hazards.** VF/asystole/ROSC from K, temperature and catecholamines are timers and weights, with no electrophysiological substrate behind them. They decide when the patient arrests and whether resuscitation works.
5. **L2-09 regional flow / lactate.** Lactate and hepatic clearance in shock come from a CO-ratio ramp tuned to the class III lactate band. Lactate is the main shock and sepsis teaching signal. The renal pair L2-06/L2-07 (fixed urine composition; fitted EABV exponent; two separate angiotensin states) comes next.

Note for the orchestrator: L2-61's tag split would turn this inventory into a code-level gate (a [FIT] without a source or brief id fails CI).

**Top 5**
1. **TIME-01**: the 70-s τ now slows the ROSC EtCO2 rise. The repo's own research (§4.6) says the rise is abrupt, within 2–4 breaths, with an overshoot. This hits the one capnography sign clinicians use to detect ROSC, and it comes from a phenomenological φ multiplier rather than CO2 delivery by pulmonary blood flow.
2. **TIME-12**: post-shock HR and BP are scripted linear ramps on MANUAL targets, even in MODELED physiology. Real ROSC often surges and then turns hypotensive.
3. **X-03**: about 35 lung-pathology effect sizes have a sourced direction but an invented magnitude: pleural-fluid compliance and pressure, the tension ceiling, ARDS/pneumonia PVR. They drive airway pressures, SpO2 and haemodynamics in the core respiratory scenarios.

5. **X-05 / X-08**: ondansetron QTc runs about 3× the FDA label (+14 ms vs +5.6 ms at twice the dose). Per-shock cardioversion success of 0.8 ignores Mittal 2000's 68 % at the first shock (70 J). Both are cheap sourced replacements.
