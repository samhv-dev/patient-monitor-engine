Notes:
- **AF NIBP bias: ruled not acceptable, now fixed.** Before the fix, 30 AF cycles at 75/min read DBP +14.7 ± 14.3 and MAP +6.1 ± 12.2 mmHg. The cause was as the orchestrator suspected. One large post-pause pulse became the envelope peak. Deflation then stopped as soon as two raw steps fell below Rd·peak, which is the DBP point itself. The low-side width was fitted from ratios against that inflated peak, so DBP read high. The fix, in `src/l3/nibp/nibp.ts`:
  - a step's amplitude is the mean of all its pulses (the matched pair in sinus);
  - an empty step waits 1.5 × the longest of the last 5 pulse intervals;
  - the envelope is [1 2 1]/4-smoothed across steps for both the stop rule and the inversion, and the kernel's STEP² is removed from the fitted widths;
  - deflation stops only when two smoothed steps are below 0.6·peak.

  After the fix, AF 75 over 30 cycles on seeds 1/2/3/4/5/6/9 gives SBP bias −0.1 to +4.0 and DBP bias +1.6 to +4.2. SDs are 5.6–8.4, except seed 2 (SBP SD 10.2, DBP SD 11.0). AF cycles now take 47–60 s, against ≈ 28 s in sinus, a larger gap than before (49.1 vs 27.8 on the seed-9 series above; it was 31.6 vs 28.8). AF 110 reads within 1.5 mmHg. The AF test in `hemo-nibp.test.ts` now asserts \|bias\| ≤ 6 and SD ≤ 10 for SBP, DBP and MAP. The first-cycle mean rose from 31.8 to 33.9 s, still inside 25–35 s but with less margin.
