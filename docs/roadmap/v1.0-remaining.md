## Not in v1.0 (moved to v2.0)

- The autonomic hub and the cardiovascular reflexes, the perfusion floor and decompensation (was "FU-12"): see
  [v2.0/evidence/cardiac-reflexes-audit.md](v2.0/evidence/cardiac-reflexes-audit.md). **Open decision:** whether a
  minimal subset must still go into v1.0 because v1 otherwise teaches shock wrongly (an awake patient who loses half
  the blood volume settles at a mean pressure of 63 mmHg and never arrests). Decide after the coupling ledger exists.
- Endocrine and thermal Part B (lactate in sepsis and the remaining cells).
- Paediatric and obstetric physiology; laboratory values; coagulation.

## Known limits to state in the v1.0 release notes

Education only, not a medical device. Adults only. Known-miss tests are listed in the test suite as `it.fails`, each
with its measured value and its target. Shock decompensation and reflex bradycardia are not modelled (above).
