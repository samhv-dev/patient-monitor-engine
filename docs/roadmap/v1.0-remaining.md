## Classroom defects (post-demo backlog)

- **Monitor start without a worker race.** Today the page waits up to 2 s (`READY_TIMEOUT_MS`, `worker-host.ts`) for the
  engine worker and otherwise runs the engine on the main thread for good; a slow worker load (the dev server, a loaded
  laptop) decides the render path by a clock. Change: start every monitor on the main thread at once, and hand over to
  the worker when it posts `ready`, at any time. The hand-over needs a snapshot: take the main-thread engine's snapshot
  at a tick boundary, restore it in the worker, keep the clock anchor and the lanes' history, then stop the main-thread
  pump, with commands sent during the hand-over queued and replayed in order. The proof: an e2e that delays the worker's
  `ready` by 5 s and checks the monitor draws from the first second, switches to `worker-*` after the delay, and loses no
  tick, command or alarm across the switch (the event log and the snapshot tick match a run without the delay).
- **A press dropped when its button is rebuilt under the pointer** (likely; WebKit seen, Chromium not ruled out). Where:
  the first Instructor view's scenario panel (`panel/scenario.ts`) rebuilds every "What happens next" button with
  `next.replaceChildren(...)` on each repaint; Instructor v2's ACTION (`teach2/action.ts`, `sync()`) keeps each row's
  button but moves every row into new `<li>` elements whenever the order changes. A click whose press and release
  straddle that repaint lands on a removed or moved node and the step never happens. Evidence: `ui-polish-a` UI-A #5 at
  1920 × 1080 on WebKit passed 9 of 10 runs (4 November, PR #260's runs: "Premedicate" did not take, "Induce" never
  appeared); in CI on WebKit, `teach2-fit` "at treated", "at recovering" and "recovering-2" left rows "ready" 10 s after
  the click (PR #258 runs 41804425424, 41811092856; PR #191 run 41786137019). Change: update the buttons in place and
  keep their nodes stable across repaints; move only the rows whose position changed. The proof: an e2e that presses a
  step while a repaint is forced between press and release, on Chromium and WebKit, 20 runs each, every press taken.
