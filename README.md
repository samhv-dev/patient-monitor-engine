# patient-monitor-engine 1.0.0-rc.4 (pre-release) — static site

The built teaching app of `main` 649ab03c (2026-10-31, "kit E"): the tagged rc.4 plus sixteen merged changes from the clinical owner's first-use list. Instructor: one Instructor entry, with today's view or Instructor v2 chosen in Settings (#204); in v2 the strip's inline box confirms a return or a jump (#202), past stages can be returned to after a reload (#205), finished steps are grouped under their stage (#206) and a /1 card's spoke states read as actions of their hub stage (#215); with no case, Instructor v2 shows three cards (#210); the Case briefing covers the host and every phone for a minute when a card loads (#211); "Finish the case" stops the clock, saves the debrief to the browser's archive and restarts the card for the next resident (#214); toasts rest on the dock (#226); the education line leaves the working views (#212). Monitor: the learners' return controls no longer cover the ECG (#203); the ECG auto-gain holds through ectopy (#213). Model: the MH sympathetic surge constricts the skin, so the MH heat stays in the core (#209); Cool is offered only above 38.5 °C and cooling stops at 38 °C (#216). Record and debrief: a stage entry is attributed by the transition the driver took (#217); a step pressed in one state of the MH hub is done, not overdue at the next (#222). Not in this build: #207 (IBP lane scales; post-demo by the owner's ruling) and #208 (escape rhythms at their own interval; its tamponade band must be re-recorded first). A build for the owner's rehearsal, not a release; the version line still reads 1.0.0-rc.4 by design. For education only:
not a medical device and not clinically validated. Source, release notes and known limitations:
https://github.com/samhv-dev/patient-monitor-engine (see `CHANGELOG.md` and `docs/guides/known-deviations.md`).

Learners' phones need the classroom relay of the local kit, built from a source checkout with
`node scripts/showcase/make-bundle.mjs` (Node.js ≥ 22); this static copy on GitHub Pages cannot pair phones.

`VERSION.txt` names the exact build. Licence: MIT (`LICENSE`); third-party notices in `NOTICES.md` and `LICENSES/`.
